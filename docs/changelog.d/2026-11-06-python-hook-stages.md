Added: setup and teardown output is saved under `<experiment>/.labnotes/logs/<hook>/<stamp>/` (`stdout.log`, `stderr.log`, `metadata.json`) instead of being discarded. When an experiment has hooks, the run TUI shows setup, runs, and teardown as stages across the top, gives each hook a screen with its status, elapsed time, and live logs, and advances through the stages on its own until you move with `[` and `]`.
