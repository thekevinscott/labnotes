**Changed** — When an outside edit merges into unsaved edits in the `labnotes server` note editor, a notice says so without taking focus. If the edits will not merge, the editor keeps your text and the outside version is saved beside the note as `<name> (Conflicted copy YYYYMMDDHHMM).md`, through the new `POST /experiments/{name}/notes/{note}/conflict`. Continuous typing now saves at least every 2 seconds.
