**Fixed** `labnotes experiments <name> run` opens its TUI at once and lists each startup step (starting the daemon, connecting, reading the experiment config, finalizing lost runs, snapshotting the repo, starting the run) while the daemon works. A start that takes longer than 10 seconds no longer fails with "No daemon is running": the client waits as long as the daemon is alive, the daemon no longer gives up on preparation after 30 seconds, and a slow reply no longer deletes the live daemon's socket.
